The Hoje view includes a club distance leaderboard for the current week, month, year, or all recorded time. It ranks only distances attached to completed training sessions, handles tied positions, shows the first ten positions plus the signed-in athlete when applicable, and excludes future sessions, inactive memberships, and private accounts. Athletes control their own leaderboard visibility; guardians control it for their dependants.

Wearable activity providers share an extensible adapter contract for connection status, bounded recent sync, backfill, webhook ingestion, and disconnection. Provider names and remote identifiers are not closed database enums, so Polar, Garmin, Strava, WHOOP, and later providers can use the same persistence model. No provider-specific OAuth, external API call, visible integration UI, automatic activity matching, or login-triggered sync is part of this foundation.

Training plans and sessions are scoped to teams or programmes, with teams preferred as the existing group model. Sessions are visible from active matching memberships without individual assignment. The structured-planning foundation, guarded by the `structured_training_planning` feature flag and administrator-only by default, reuses those durable plan/session identities while adding training groups, dated weeks with an explicit persisted season, rest/competition/logistics entries, ordered hybrid segments (including gym, water, run, bike, ergometer, mobility, and games), and ordered warm-up/main/cool-down/technique blocks. Structured water segments add continuous, interval, fartlek, technique, starts, race-simulation, technical/tactical and custom methods; nested repeat groups with recovery at the prescribed level; mixed exact or estimated duration/distance efforts; cadence and readable kayak-polo drill metadata; free text; and a session-distance target suitable for “continue until 12 km” cooldowns. Planned effort, recovery, distance, and time-by-intensity totals are derived without inventing pace and are labelled exact, estimated, or unknown. Administrators manage club-defined, revisioned kayak/canoe intensity profiles and zones; new revisions copy the previous zones, historical blocks retain their selected revision, R7 remains duration-relative, and no disputed R5 default is seeded. Structured gym segments add straight sets, circuits and supersets; repeated rounds and recovery; ordered exercise prescriptions; resistance through kg, %1RM, body weight, band, RPE, RIR or coach instruction; execution intent and tempo; and optional hybrid start offsets, transition time and equipment notes. Session modality summaries are derived from their ordered segments. Coaches can save immutable block, segment or session snapshots as private routines or share them only within the source plan's authorized programme/team scope; the library supports modality, objective, method, tag and text filtering with a readable preview. Routine insertion and direct block, session, day or full-week copying reconstruct independent relational drafts—including water prescriptions, nested steps, and profile revision—and append lightweight provenance, so later edits cannot rewrite the source. Athletes see plans from active group memberships; named guardians receive the same read-only plan view for their active minor dependants, with relationship, age, and membership eligibility recalculated on each request. In-scope coaches can target bounded omit, replace, add, or field-override variations at an athlete, subgroup, or date-effective temporary crew; crew capacity follows the configured K2/K4/C2/C4-style craft code, individual rules outrank group rules, same-priority matches are explicit conflicts, and a resolved per-athlete preview revalidates session-date membership before publication. Perception feedback and wearable-derived planned/realized load remain later tickets in the training epic. Administrators and in-scope coaches can edit active future sessions with optimistic concurrency; moving a session to another plan requires access to both plans and becomes locked after the first athlete outcome. They can also irreversibly cancel a future session with an actor, timestamp, and trimmed reason while preserving existing outcomes and replacement links. Cancelled sessions remain visible across athlete, staff, calendar, Hoje, and programme-agenda surfaces, reject outcome and distance changes, cannot be selected as new replacements, and do not contribute to the distance leaderboard. Athletes report `COMPLETED`, `MISSED`, or `REPLACED` outcomes; a replacement must identify another visible active session and a short reason. A completed outcome can store an optional distance as integer metres, and the athlete can later correct or remove that distance while the session remains active. Staff attendance and check-in are not part of the training workflow.

Equipment inventory entries support administrator-uploaded JPEG, PNG, or WebP photos stored privately and displayed through short-lived signed URLs. Photo replacements are included in equipment audit history.

Authenticated person surfaces use the profile avatar endpoint for account context, guardian/dependant views, the administrator member directory, event responses/check-ins, and the distance leaderboard. The endpoint serves only a current-consent photo for an active member (or an inactive member to an administrator) and otherwise returns an initials image without exposing profile data.
